Measuring the Depth of LLM Unlearning via Activation Patching

Jaeung Lee1,2, Dohyun Kim1, Jaemin Jo1

1Sungkyunkwan University    2Samsung Research

arXiv Code 🤗 Data

We present the Unlearning Depth Score (UDS), a mechanistic metric that quantifies the depth of unlearning by measuring how much target knowledge is recoverable through two-stage activation patching. This page provides: (i) an interactive walkthrough of the UDS pipeline, (ii) a meta-evaluation comparing 20 metrics on faithfulness and robustness, and (iii) per-method benchmark results across 150 unlearned models. The results below benchmark UDS on the TOFU [12] dataset (forget10) using Llama-3.2-1B-Instruct and the Open-Unlearning [18] framework.

Method

Preparing the Input

Construct a prompt from question and answer prefix, and mark the entity span to evaluate.

A Stage 1: Baselining

Patch retain model's hidden states into the full model at each layer.

Log-probs are compared token-by-token under teacher forcing.

Large drops reveal layers encoding knowledge the retain model lacks.

B Stage 2: Quantification

Repeat with the unlearned model's hidden states.

Drops matching Stage 1 = erased; near-zero = knowledge intact.

C Score Aggregation

① Filter layers that significantly encode the target knowledge.

② Compute per-layer erasure ratio from the two stages.

③ Aggregate into a final score: 0 (intact) → 1 (erased).

Meta-evaluation

We evaluate 20 metrics to measure how reliable each unlearning metric is, using the Open-Unlearning benchmark framework.

Faithfulness: How well each metric separates knowledge-present (P, 30 models) vs knowledge-absent (N, 30 models), measured by AUC-ROC.
Robustness: Stability of each metric under post-hoc perturbations (4-bit quantization, 1-epoch relearning).

Formulas
Faithfulness = AUC-ROC over P/N pools (30 knowledge-present vs 30 knowledge-absent models)
Robustness = HM(Q, R) — symmetric (bidirectional)
Quantization (Q) $= 1 - \dfrac{|m_{\text{after}} - m_{\text{before}}|}{|m_{\text{before}}| + |m_{\text{after}}|}$ — penalizes both recovery and destruction after 4-bit NF4 quantization
Relearning (R) $= 1 - \dfrac{|\Delta_{\text{unl}} - \Delta_{\text{ret}}|}{|\Delta_{\text{unl}}| + |\Delta_{\text{ret}}|}$,   $\Delta = m_{\text{after}} - m_{\text{before}}$ — penalizes both over- and under-recovery relative to retain
Direction: standard metrics use raw values (higher = more knowledge); UDS and $s_*$ are inverted ($m = 1 - \text{value}$) so that higher $m$ = more knowledge before Q/R computation.
Filtering: utility $\geq 0.8$ + per-metric faithfulness-threshold (150 models $\to$ per-metric filtered subset).
Overall = HM(Faithfulness, Robustness)
All metrics are oriented so that ↑ higher = better. Metrics where higher raw value indicates more knowledge (e.g., Prob, ROUGE) are inverted before evaluation; UDS and $s_*$ are already in the correct direction.
Metric Categories
Memorization (5) — Does the model still retain memorized target knowledge?
ES [1] (Extraction Strength) — fraction of answer extractable via greedy decoding. $1 - k/T$, $k$ = earliest match position
EM [2] (Exact Memorization) — token-level position match ratio. $\sum \mathbb{1}(\text{pred}_t = \text{label}_t)\, /\, T$
Prob — geometric mean of per-token probabilities. $\exp\!\bigl(-(1/T)\sum \text{CE}_t\bigr)$
ParaProb — geometric mean of Prob across paraphrased answer variants.
Truth Ratio — normalized correct vs. incorrect probability. $p_c / (p_c + p_w)$,   $p = \exp(-\text{avg loss})$
Generation (3) — Can the model reproduce target knowledge in free-form text?
ROUGE-L recall between model generation and ground-truth answer under different prompting strategies.
ROUGE — standard prompt.
Para. ROUGE — paraphrased prompt.
Jailbreak ROUGE — adversarial prompt (prefixed with “Sure, here is the answer:”).
MIA (8) — Membership Inference Attack. Per-sample member/non-member classification [6].
Raw (4) — AUC-based:
MIA-LOSS [9] — average cross-entropy. $\frac{1}{T}\sum_t \mathcal{L}_t$
MIA-ZLib [1] — loss normalized by compression length. $\ell\, /\, |\texttt{zlib}(x)|$,   $\ell = \frac{1}{T}\sum_t \mathcal{L}_t$
MIA-Min-K [7] — mean of bottom-$k$% log-probs. $-\frac{1}{\lceil kT\rceil}\sum_{t \in \mathcal{B}_k} \log p_t,\;\; k{=}0.4$
MIA-Min-K++ [8] — standardized Min-K. $z_t = (\log p_t - \mu_t) / \sigma_t$, then Min-K on $z_t$
Normalized (4, denoted $s_*$) — MUSE PrivLeak-style rescaling using retain as anchor [10]:
$\text{normalized}_* = |\text{AUC}_{\text{model}} - \text{AUC}_{\text{retain}}|\, /\, \text{AUC}_{\text{retain}}$
$s_* = 1 - \min(\text{normalized}_*,\; 1)$  (inverted; 1.0 = erased, 0.0 = large deviation from retain)
sLOSS · sZLib · sMin-K · sMin-K++
Representation (4) — Measure knowledge erasure at the representation level. All use the retain model as reference.
CKA [3] — representational similarity via kernel alignment, normalized against the full–retain anchor per layer.
$\text{erasure}_l=\text{clip}\!\left(\dfrac{\text{CKA}(H_{\text{unl}},H_{\text{ret}})_l-\text{CKA}(H_{\text{full}},H_{\text{ret}})_l}{1-\text{CKA}(H_{\text{full}},H_{\text{ret}})_l+\epsilon},\,0,\,1\right),\;\;w_l=1-\text{CKA}(H_{\text{full}},H_{\text{ret}})_l,\;\;\text{score}=\dfrac{\sum_l w_l\cdot \text{erasure}_l}{\sum_l w_l}$  
Logit Lens [4] — decodes each layer’s hidden states through the full model’s frozen decoder (LayerNorm + lm_head) to measure decodable knowledge per layer.
$k_{m,l} = \text{logprob}(\text{entity}\mid H^l_m \to \text{decoder}_{\text{full}})$,   $d_{m,l} = k_{\text{full},l} - k_{m,l}$
Fisher Masked [5] — diagonal Fisher Information with top-$p$% parameter masking. Focuses on parameters where retain has higher sensitivity than full on the forget set. $p$ ∈ {0.01%, 0.1%, 1%}.
$a_i = \max(F_{\text{ret},i} - F_{\text{full},i},\; 0)$,   $M_l$ = top $p$% of $a_i$,   $\text{erasure}_l = 1 - \text{clip}(\text{excess}_{\text{unl}} / \text{excess}_{\text{full}},\; 0,\; 1)$
UDS (Ours) — Measures whether knowledge remains recoverable via activation patching.
Notation: $x$ = input prompt, $y = (y_1,\dots,y_T)$ = entity span tokens, $M_{\text{full}}$ = full model, $M_S$ = source model ($S1$=retain, $S2$=unlearned).
$s^{\text{full}}_t = \log p_{M_{\text{full}}}(y_t \mid x, y_{1:t{-}1})$  (full model log-prob of entity token $y_t$)
$s^{S}_t = \log p^{\text{patch}(M_S)}_{M_{\text{full}}}(y_t \mid x, y_{1:t{-}1})$  (log-prob after patching layer $l$ with source activations)
$\Delta^{S}_{l} = \frac{1}{T}\sum_{t} (s^{\text{full}}_t - s^{S}_t)$  (mean log-prob degradation at layer $l$; larger = source lacks more knowledge)
$\text{FT}_i = \{l : \Delta^{S1}_{i,l} > \tau\}$  ($\tau{=}0.05$, filters noise: layers where retain genuinely lacks target knowledge)
$\text{UDS}_i = \dfrac{\sum_{l\in\text{FT}} \Delta^{S1}_{i,l} \cdot \text{clip}(\Delta^{S2}_{i,l} / \Delta^{S1}_{i,l},\; 0,\; 1)}{\sum_{l\in\text{FT}} \Delta^{S1}_{i,l}}$  ($\Delta^{S1}$-weighted average of per-layer erasure ratios)
$\text{UDS} = \frac{1}{N}\sum_i \text{UDS}_i$

Benchmark

We evaluate 152 models (8 methods × varying hyperparameters × 2 epochs + full + retain) across three axes. All unlearned model checkpoints are from the Open-Unlearning framework.

Memorization: How much target knowledge was forgotten. (↑ higher = more forgotten)
Privacy: How well sensitive information from the forget set is protected from being extracted. (↑ higher = better protected)
Utility: How well the model retains general capabilities on non-target knowledge. (↑ higher = better retention)
Overall: Harmonic mean of all three axes. (↑ higher = better)

UDS is incorporated into the Privacy axis as Privacy = HM(MIA, UDS), capturing both statistical (MIA) and mechanistic (UDS) aspects.
Formulas
Memorization:
Mem. $= \text{HM}(1{-}\text{ES},\; 1{-}\text{EM},\; 1{-}\text{ParaProb},\; 1{-}\text{TruthRatio})$
ES=Extraction Strength, EM=Exact Memorization, ParaProb=Paraphrase Probability, TruthRatio=Truth Ratio
Privacy:
MIA $= \text{HM}(s_{\text{LOSS}},\; s_{\text{ZLib}},\; s_{\text{Min-K}},\; s_{\text{Min-K++}})$
$s_* = 1 - \min(|\text{AUC} - \text{AUC}_{\text{ret}}|\, /\, \text{AUC}_{\text{ret}},\; 1)$
Privacy = HM(MIA, UDS)
Utility:
ModelUtility (MU) = HM(retain_Prob, retain_ROUGE, retain_TruthRatio, ra_Prob, ra_ROUGE, ra_TruthRatio, wf_Prob, wf_ROUGE, wf_TruthRatio)
retain=Retain set, ra=Real Authors, wf=World Facts
Fluency = generation fluency score
Utility = HM(MU, Fluency), then normalized: $\text{Utility}_{\text{rel}} = \text{Utility}\, /\, \text{Utility}_{\text{full(epoch)}}$
Overall:
Overall $= \text{HM}(\text{Mem.},\; \text{Privacy},\; \text{Utility}_{\text{rel}})$
